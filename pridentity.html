Case Study

Pridentity

A Platform for Queer Media

Overview

Pridentity is a mobile platform designed to uplift queer voices: music, novels, podcasts, and programs that let queer people access the stories that speak to their own lives. Rather than searching scattered platforms, Pridentity is a curated destination where discovering, reviewing, and sharing queer content feels natural and joyful.

Problem

Queer audiences struggle to find LGBTQ+ representation in mainstream media platforms. Content is scattered across services with no dedicated discovery experience.

Solution

A curated mobile platform dedicated to queer media that surfaces LGBTQ+ content across formats in one cohesive, community-driven experience.

User Research

Through one-on-one interviews with queer media consumers, I identified key pain points: difficulty discovering genre-specific content, lack of a trusted rating system driven by people who share their identity, and no way to signal media interests through profile labels. Research also surfaced a strong desire for social features — messaging and shared watchlists.

Low-Fidelity

Information architecture and quick paper sketches to test screen flows and navigation structures before committing to visual design.

Mid-Fidelity

Established layout patterns across core screens while locking in the Pridentity logo mark and colour direction — a deep purple and rainbow accent palette signalling identity and pride.

Hi-Fidelity

With the visual language firmly set, I refined every screen to highlight queer media discovery and community features. Each group below focuses on a distinct user goal.

Browse through a diverse range of queer media

Review and rating system driven by the community

Message and view other users' activity

Show off your "Pridentity" through profile labels and interests displays

Post your own media ratings or reviews

Usability Testing

Conducted moderated usability sessions with participants who identified within the LGBTQ+ community. Key findings informed refinements to the navigation hierarchy and the profile label selection flow.

Moving Forward

Next I plan to expand Pridentity into a complete user journey, building further social and community discovery features. The project has deepened my conviction in designing spaces where underrepresented communities can feel genuinely seen.
