Case Study

FADS

All of FADS’ info and creative identity in one place

fadsatucdavis.framer.websiteVisit ↗

Role

Co-Web Designer + Developer

Tools

Figma, Framer

Overview

The Fashion and Design Society (FADS) is a student club at UC Davis. This website brings everything about the club into one place: who we are, what we make, and how to get involved. It also promotes FADS to future collaborators and helps the club grow. Through fashion-forward visuals and game-like interactions, the site expresses the creative spirit, community, and identity that make FADS, FADS.

Problem

Club info, work, and ways to join were scattered across platforms and hard to find.

Solution

One creative hub that makes FADS easy to find, join, and collaborate with.

Before

FADS already had a website, but it had been abandoned and untouched for years. Almost no one knew it existed, and it no longer reflected the club's identity, community, or opportunities.

Goals

01

Put everything in one place

All club info and events in one place, without searching across multiple sources.

02

Make getting involved easy

Clear paths for students to join, attend events, or reach out.

03

Show who we are

Showcase our club's creative spirit and playful, fashion-forward personality.

04

Grow our reach

Show brands and creatives what FADS is about, opening doors to collaboration and growth.
